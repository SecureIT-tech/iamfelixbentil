About Me

Researching secure, private, and connected systems.

My interests sit at the intersection of cybersecurity, communications, and privacy. I am particularly interested in wireless security, quantum networking, cryptographic technologies, and how technical systems affect privacy and trust.

This site brings together my research, class projects, presentations, and professional work.
